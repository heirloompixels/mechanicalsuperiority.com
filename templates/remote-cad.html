Design and CAD

Send the model. Get back one that's easy to change, and parts that fit.

Design help doesn't need a shop visit. Inventors, startups and companies without an engineer on staff send an idea, a sketch or a model, and it's worked out together by email and phone.

How it went for a customer in Virginia

A set of bicycle brake adapters, 2024.

  1. His design

    The customer had models of his own that were hard to change.

  2. Rebuilt

    Mark remodeled them so that a change took minutes, not a rebuild.

  3. Daily changes

    A revision a day, printed in plastic by the customer at home.

  4. Proven

    Fitted on the bike until the geometry was right.

  5. Made

    Machined from 6061-T6 aluminum by a CNC manufacturer, with Mark working between them.

  6. Fit

    First time, because the plastic had already been wrong on their behalf.

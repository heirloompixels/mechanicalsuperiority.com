Fort Collins, Colorado. Much of the design work is done remotely, so where you
are matters less than you'd think.

**What helps:** whatever you've got. A drawing, a model, a photo of the broken
part, a sketch, or just a description of what the thing needs to do. Photos
with a tape measure in frame beat a paragraph. Quantity (one is normal),
material if you know it, and when you need it. If you don't know the material
or the tolerances, that's a normal question and part of what you're hiring
for.

**Files:** SolidWorks or Fusion native, or STEP, IGES, DXF and PDF.

**Other shops:** if you're full, missing a capability, or need a second
source, get in touch. Overflow work and referrals go both directions.

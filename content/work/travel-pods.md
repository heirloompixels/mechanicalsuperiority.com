This is the job that started Mark's partnership with Ehpro, and the one that
shows what the two shops do together: an engineer who can measure a physical
part and turn it into models a structural analyst can use, next to a
fabricator that can build them in numbers. Mark calls it the best example of
what his skills add up to: design, reverse engineering, CAM, machining and
hands-on assembly, all on one project.

Some of the pods are painted for their squadrons, and some for the USAF
Thunderbirds.

kind = "Reverse engineering and production, with EhPro"
what = "When a fighter jet needs to deploy or go somewhere for training, there is an extensive list of things that need to go with it: toolboxes, equipment, luggage, etc... The maintenance crews pack much of these items into Travel Pods to make sure that it all goes with the jet. EhPro has decades of experience refurbishing used Travel Pods and knows them inside and out. But when an opportunity came along to build brand new ones from scratch, it came with a requirement for CAD models and shop drawings so that the details of the construction could be confirmed and analyzed by the Air Force. At the time EhPro had no in-house CAD capabilities. So they turned to Mechanical Superiority for help, and a harmonious partnership was born."
done = "A prototype pod was shipped to Mechanical Superiority in Colorado, and the pod was measured extensively, with minimal disassembly as-required. Combined with remote communication (including some emailed hand drawings back and forth), this process yielded accurate CAD models and assembly, and a full set of construction-ready shop drawings for EhPro. The customer was extremely satisfied with the quality and usability of the shop drawings, and the Air Force got the CAD models they needed to confirm and approve the design so that the customer could massively upgrade their product line. The CAD models also allowed EhPro to upgrade their manufacturing capacity: With the support of Mechanical Superiority's CAD/CAM capability, EhPro decided to acquire a Haas CNC Gantry mill so that the major components of the pods could be made with substantially increased precision. The result has been a radical reduction in the unit production time of the pods, as well as a better-looking and more consistent end product. Mechanical Superiority is now partnered with EhPro and also assists in the construction of the pods (riveting and TIG welding of the main bodies). At risk of understatement: Not many engineering partners bring this much to the table."

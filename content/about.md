It is the only way for us to learn what works in real life, and what only works in a CAD model. Unfortunately the apprentice-style of learning has largely been abandoned in modern times, in favor of an out-of-touch emphasis on academics and theory in the Universities where most engineers are trained. Beyond my education I have also been lucky enough to receive education and mentorship (and my fair share of “constructive criticism”) from many of the guys on the ground; machinists, fabricators, millwrights, mechanics, etc... who actually built the real systems that we were asking them to construct. I have since undertaken to learn the arts of machining, fabrication, and wrenching for myself through seeking mentorship, as well as self-education. This hands-on experience has been invaluable.'''

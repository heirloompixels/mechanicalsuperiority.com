Ford 641 tractor frame

  • The projectA local customer owned a beautifully maintained Ford 641 Tractor, and was looking for a shade structure to be added to improve the comfort level for mowing pastures on hot days. Initially, the customer wanted Mechanical Superiority to modify a boring off-the-shelf solution made from painted steel and plastic. With his creativity and advanced skills, Mark worked up a concept for a custom aluminum frame and matching canvas top. The customer was immediately sold on the idea based on the improved aesthetics and maintaining more of a classic look for the tractor. Plus, who else is ever going to have a super cool aluminum space frame shade structure on their old tractor?
  • The resultsMechanical Superiority built and installed the one-off custom solution for the satisfied customer. To this day, everybody who sees the shade frame is immediately impressed by the quality of construction and the strength/durability of the structure. Classic looks, with space-age performance!
